Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Data Not Collected. Kahroba: RAW Camera does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking

The app does not require an account or login. It includes no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs or tracking. This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers.

Photos and edits stay on your device

Photos you capture and edits you make stay on your device. If you choose to save a photo, it is written to the Photos library on your device.

Camera and Photo Library permissions

Kahroba uses Camera access only to capture photos, and Photo Library access only to save photos when you ask the app to do so.

Location in photo metadata

An optional “Include location” setting can embed GPS coordinates in the photo file metadata on your device. This setting is off by default. Location data is never sent anywhere by the app.

Children

Kahroba does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
